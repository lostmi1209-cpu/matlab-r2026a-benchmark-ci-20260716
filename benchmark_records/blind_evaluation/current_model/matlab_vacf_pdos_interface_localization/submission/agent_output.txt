Implemented a self-contained MATLAB R2026a solution. It reads the public CSV/HDF5 inputs, corrects velocity dimension order from known frame/atom/component counts, removes mass-weighted center-of-mass motion and per-channel time means, computes unbiased zero-padded FFT VACF and symmetric-Hann one-sided unit-integral PDOS, derives all required peak and band metrics, performs the required heterostructure Welch t-test and Hedges g calculation, and writes the two CSV files plus the JSON summary in UTF-8. Numerical outputs are derived from the supplied inputs and checked for NaN/Inf.
